How the study works

  1. Fit

    Two surveys begin at home. Each tests compass moves and records where they lead, including blocked moves. A split is proposed only for the prespecified junction symbol observed at distinct positions.

  2. Select

    Compare the fitted candidates on two selection traversals, then freeze the choice. The control gives both junctions identically labeled exits in each direction.

  3. Evaluate

    Score both candidates on four final traversals. Then replay six landmark routes with a 24-action limit. The rotated cases test the same layouts in another orientation.

Research behind the question

A renormalising generative model (RGM) groups recurring blocks of observations and sequences across fixed spatial and temporal scales. Its “path” is a latent transition pattern. Here a route is a sequence of executed compass moves, and the learned structure is a spatial place split. Learning multi-step events is a separate research question. RGM, Algorithm 1 · Pong example

AIMAPP proposes places from local observations and updates directed connections through experienced movement. It motivates the distinction between discovering a place and learning how to return to it. AIMAPP, §3.5 · Goal-reaching benchmark, Table 5
