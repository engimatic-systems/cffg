# Preserve proper recurrence statistics under concentrated Gaussian beliefs

## Background in Plain English

The source-heterogeneous calibration reached a converged hidden-state solve and
then rejected the next recurrence-learning update as improper. Both affected
fits returned `diverged`. The failed candidate update was atomic, so the last
accepted parameters remained usable for diagnosis, but the coordinate did not
satisfy the calibration convergence contract.

The error comes from the declared Pólya–Gamma recurrence learning path. A
deterministic replay identified `switch447` as the only failing factor. Its
Gaussian drive was proper and positive definite. Reconstructing its covariance
as a second moment minus the outer product of its mean introduced a symmetry
error of `1.1102230246251565e-16`, above the dtype-scaled tolerance
`3.966749545204547e-17`, so the derived learning readout rejected a valid
belief.

This ticket fixes the numerical invariant before cff-s6rs can select margins or
budgets. It does not relax a true impropriety, change the recurrence prior to
make the experiment pass, or relabel divergence as budget exhaustion.

## Outcomes / Acceptance Criteria

1. Recurrence statistic diagnostics identify the failing factor, failed
   predicate, relevant minimum eigenvalue or scalar bound, and dtype-scaled
   tolerance when a statistic is improper.
2. A mathematically proper Gaussian belief with a large mean and small positive
   covariance produces finite, proper Pólya–Gamma statistics without subtractive
   cancellation in float64 and float32.
3. A truly non-finite, indefinite, unnormalized, or negative-support input still
   fails loudly; the fix does not add an absolute ridge, floor, or permissive
   clamp.
4. Exact categorical zeros remain valid support boundaries when the remaining
   mass is finite, nonnegative, and normalized. Vacuous or improper Gaussian
   inputs retain their documented failure behavior.
5. The registered source-heterogeneous calibration coordinate for seed 3101,
   replicate 0 no longer fails with `recurrence_link statistic is improper`.
   Each tier either meets the registered outer convergence rule or reports an
   honest non-converged status that cannot support calibration.
6. The hostile regression exercises at least 1,279 factors with the same
   large-mean, small-covariance mechanism, in addition to the compact
   factor-local case. Existing recurrence message, energy, learning, and classic
   rSLDS regressions pass.
7. The architecture documentation explains how recurrence learning obtains a
   covariance without subtracting large, nearly equal second-moment terms and
   where factor-local propriety diagnostics are owned.
   Any remaining numerical boundary is recorded in `docs/LIMITATIONS.md`.
8. The final short suite passes once after the last CFFG code change. No final
   rSLDS seed or immutable dual-family preregistration is generated by this
   ticket.

## Methodological Suggestions

These suggestions are optional. The implementer may choose another sound method
that satisfies the outcomes.

- Carry the covariance computed directly from Gaussian precision through the
  recurrence readout, then form each logit second moment as the squared mean
  plus the covariance contraction.
- If a subtraction remains necessary, scale its tolerance from the uncancelled
  second-moment and mean-outer-product operands, not from the already-cancelled
  covariance.
- Keep outer parameter rollback atomic. Add parameter damping or backtracking
  only if stable moment extraction shows a genuine fixed-point overshoot.
- Do not strengthen the Pólya–Gamma prior merely to make this coordinate pass;
  that would change the scientific model.

## Definitions

- **Pólya–Gamma recurrence statistic:** A sufficient statistic used by the
  conjugate update for the recurrent logistic gates.
- **Subtractive cancellation:** Loss of significant digits when two large,
  nearly equal floating-point values are subtracted to obtain a small result.
- **Proper Gaussian belief:** A finite Gaussian whose covariance is positive
  semidefinite within a tolerance derived from its dtype and operand scale.
- **Atomic rollback:** Retaining the last accepted parameters when a candidate
  learning update fails, while still reporting the fit as diverged.

### 2026-08-18 — deterministic cancellation regression

Question → Can the suspected covariance cancellation be isolated without
rerunning the 1,600-step calibration coordinate?

Answer → Yes. With `mean = [1e8, 3e7]` and true covariance
`[[0.25, 0.5], [0.5, 1.5]]`, the true minimum eigenvalue is
`0.0746094703208939`.
After forming the second moment and reconstructing covariance through
`second - outer(mean, mean)`, float64 produces `[[0, 0.5], [0.5, 1.5]]`, whose
minimum eigenvalue is `-0.1513878188659973`; the dtype-scaled PSD tolerance is
`1.0658141036401503e-14`. Uniform categorical probabilities and zero recurrence
coefficients make every other `weight_stats` predicate benign. Broadcasting
that row to 1,279 factors gives the uncomfortable-scale regression without
building a trajectory graph.

The converged fit's finite Bethe free energy rules out invalid categorical
edges, non-PD drive precision, non-finite coordinate outputs, and materially
negative stable logit second at that state because recurrence energy returns
`+inf` for those conditions. `weight_stats` then performs the extra covariance
subtraction. At this stage the exact production factor and measured values
still required an instrumented calibration-only replay because the artifact did
not retain per-factor flags; that replay is recorded below.

Source → `src/cffg/recurrence.py:78-233,405-446`,
