# Requalify heterogeneous recovery with structured recurrence

## Background in Plain English — WHY

The structured recurrence repair is intended to correct the responsibility
bias that blocked source-heterogeneous transition recovery. Passing compact
engine oracles does not establish that capability. After the engine,
learning, and persistence work land, the experiment repository must retain
the new pair evidence and rerun only authorized development gates before any
new calibration or final freeze.

## Outcomes / Acceptance Criteria — WHAT

1. The experiment dependency pins the reviewed structured-recurrence CFFG
   commit and records its executable semantics in every new checkpoint and
   result manifest.
2. Prospective heterogeneous checkpoints retain normalized recurrence pair
   beliefs or their authenticated pair-derived at-risk/signed statistics,
   Gaussian moments, fitted parameters, observation digest, and existing
   truth-boundary identities. They contain no generating latent trajectory.
3. Compact replay tests reject legacy singleton-product evidence posing as D9,
   tampered identities, final or calibration seeds, non-finite pairs, invalid
   marginal consistency, and wrong source/stick shapes before data generation.
4. The cff-rk7i clamping ladder's fully inferred stage and the cff-0oqo
   source/gate/region diagnostic are rerun on the predeclared heterogeneous
   development seed set without changing the 0.150 caps.
5. Both initialization tiers meet worst-source transition and source-contrast
   RMSE at most 0.150 on every declared development coordinate, or the ticket
   preserves a falsifying artifact and stops without tuning.
6. The classic fixed-drive and generator goldens remain within their frozen
   development ceilings. Normalized probabilities, periodic behavior, and
   exact-zero support do not regress.
7. Result artifacts separate categorical-structure improvement from the
   remaining uncertain-drive JJ/PG gap and report pair consistency, inner and
   outer status, work, wall time, and peak RSS.
8. Historical cff-oyie, cff-rk7i, cff-nbo9, calibration, and final artifacts
   remain immutable. No final seed, adapter chain, or paper-equation control is
   run by this ticket.

## Methodological Suggestions — HOW

These suggestions are optional. The implementer may choose another sound
method.

- Start with compact schema/replay tests, then one declared seed/tier pilot.
- Reuse the existing truth-free prospective checkpoint and post-fit recovery
  diagnostic rather than creating a second truth boundary.
- Stop on the first censored, divergent, non-finite, or cap-failing declared
  arm and preserve that result before deciding on more computation.

## Future work

If every development coordinate passes, cff-0oqo may close and a separate
ticket may derive fresh calibration budgets and freeze new final evidence. If
the cap still fails, create a mechanism ticket from the retained pair evidence;
do not loosen the ceiling or tune on final seeds.

## Definitions

- **Requalification:** Repeating the declared development gates after a
  behavior change to determine whether the capability claim is now supported.
- **Truth boundary:** The rule that fitted checkpoints retain observations and
  learned state but do not retain or expose generating latent trajectories to
  inference.
- **Pair consistency:** Agreement between each pair belief's row/column sums
  and its two singleton regime beliefs.
