# Lower declared hybrid ownership into compiler claims and drafts

## Background in Plain English — WHY

D12 declarations identify the factors, shared variable copies and boundaries owned by a hybrid component. The compiler must turn those declarations into structural plans before ordinary factor grouping runs. Otherwise ordinary recurrence, mixture or equality plans can claim the same factors.

This bounded prerequisite separates graph interpretation from actual parameter resolution and public plan publication. It completes declaration-only claims and drafts beneath the existing compiler guard. The remaining cff-7sw5 work still resolves data, certifies assembled ownership and enables fixed-parameter bundles. No model scope, approximation or qualification requirement is reduced.

## Outcomes / Acceptance Criteria — WHAT

1. Explicit component declarations and owned equality incidence determine factor membership, regime/state copies, ordered separator/region indexes and adapter gather/scatter slots. Names, insertion order, model helpers and whole-model recognition do not select eligibility or membership.
2. Declared region factors and owned equalities enter claims.consumed before ordinary specialization and registered grouping. Ordinary recurrence components, mixture groups and equality groups cannot reclaim them.
3. Immutable claims/drafts preserve complete declaration identity and expose per-factor ArrayRequirement/MemberRequirement intentions using existing parameter-role conventions. They do not load or resolve arrays, publish numerical parameters, create accepted runtime state or declare learned statistics.
4. Drafts declare graph-derived ownership: one component execution/factor-energy owner for every region factor and owned equality; named region/separator/adapter objective owners; and ordinary singleton-entropy exclusions for internal edges whose two factor endpoints belong to the component. Adapter-facing ordinary edges retain their ordinary addresses and exterior connections outside those internal exclusions.
5. Draft construction checks the declarations' graph consistency but does not claim final ownership certification. cff-7sw5 must later compare resolved plans with every ordinary/specialized owner and assembled entropy table before Arena publication.
6. Registered source requirements retain every consumed factor's original blob role and source ordering. Source shape and initializer intentions survive claiming. Reading blobs, checking actual values and materializing parameter addresses remain cff-7sw5.
7. Structural tests cover renaming, factor reordering, Gaussian and categorical attachments through declared adapters, a compatible exterior factor outside the recurrent model family, multiple components, sparse support and named rejection of malformed ownership or association cuts. Larger cases vary graph incidence, label count and state dimension independently.
8. Discovery and draft construction do not load arrays, execute a solver, call Coordinate.from_sites, differentiate, lower or compile a numerical program. An undeclared model does not invoke D12 discovery. General admitted incidence is preserved; a chain-shaped solver interface cannot silently restrict the declaration contract.
9. Existing public compiler/bundle rejection and all shared execution guards remain intact. No final Arena with D12 parameters or successful public D12 bundle is enabled here. Unknown future coordinate/scratch storage remains unavailable, not zero.
10. The architecture and duplicated-surface handoff below is recorded before implementation; canonical ownership documentation, focused structural checks and root's one final short-suite gate pass. Independent review starts only after the fresh author commits and pauses. Root retains closure/publication and the 1200-second suite cap.

## Definitions

- D12: the accepted hybrid-regime-ec-v1 approximation and its explicit regional ownership contract.
- Claim: a record of which compiler owner handles a factor; claims.consumed prevents another grouping from claiming it.
- Draft: structural indexes and array requirements prepared before data is loaded.
- Equality incidence: graph connections that identify declared copies of one variable.
- Adapter: a declared connection between a hybrid boundary and an ordinary categorical or Gaussian copy; gather/scatter are its input/output slot addresses.
- Entropy exclusion: an ordinary edge correction omitted because the component objective owns its replacement.
- ArrayRequirement/MemberRequirement: existing compiler records describing required array roles/shapes and intended factor addresses, before values are resolved.
- Arena: the compiler's published array and address container; member_of/log_params are its existing factor-address and parameter stores.
- CFFGZ: the portable template/data/optional-learning bundle format.
- Host/JIT and single/vmap: eager/compiled and individual/batched numerical execution surfaces, all deferred here.
