# Qualify hybrid component composition and execution parity

## Background in Plain English — WHY

The D12 implementation must remain selected when ordinary factors are attached. The retired whole-template dispatch changed approximations when a graph was renamed or extended; the new public contract needs explicit composition evidence before empirical recovery.

## Outcomes / Acceptance Criteria — WHAT

1. Three public-template fixtures retain D12 under an interior Gaussian measurement/constraint, an interior categorical likelihood/constraint, and a compatible non-rSLDS boundary factor through declared equality/adapters. Exterior mixtures use the selected cff-ulbz full-tilt binding.
2. Renaming/reordering every factor and applying supported plate scoping, edits/composition, serialization, and bundle round-trip preserve identity, membership, moments, and objective.
3. Synchronous/scheduled, host/JIT, single/vmap, and current/legacy identity handling agree on boundary messages, internal readouts, FE, support, and terminal status within declared dtype-scaled tolerances.
4. An unsupported association cut or constraint fails validation/compilation with its boundary named; no objective or approximation fallback is used. The D12 temporal counterexample remains a disclosed approximation gap, not a test that falsely demands exactness.
5. Sparse support, empty regions, signed/vacuous cavities, support changes, non-finite solve states, float32, and a 10–100x inventory case exercise the public contract. Long behavioral/globalization regressions are slow and opt-in.
6. Derived component coordinate handoff checks every production compiler/runtime/readout/persistence/estimator/export surface. README construction, ARCHITECTURE ownership, DERIVATIONS anchors, and LIMITATIONS match shipped behavior; focused tests and one final short-suite gate pass.

## Methodological Suggestions — HOW

These suggestions are optional; another sound method meeting the outcomes is welcome.

Compare against the independent tiny reference before any learned NASCAR run. Use only the public declared construction in composition fixtures.

## Future work

cff-x0if performs the only end-to-end empirical source-recovery qualification authorized by the epic after this gate; it may reject the approximation based on the preregistered recovery criteria.

## Definitions

- D12: the hybrid-regime-ec-v1 mathematical contract in docs/DERIVATIONS.md.
- EC: expectation consistency, matching declared moments between regional beliefs.
- Hybrid separator: one boundary carrying regime probabilities and regime-conditioned state moments.
- Adapter: an explicit equality region connecting a hybrid boundary to an ordinary categorical or Gaussian copy.
- JJ / xi: the Jaakkola–Jordan logistic quadratic bound and its squared auxiliary coordinate.
- FE / ledger: free energy and the single assembly of its owned energy/entropy terms.
- GFE: generalised free energy with P-substitution, excluded from D12.
- IR: serialized intermediate representation of model semantics.
- Arena / member_of: compiled array storage and the map from factors to its members.
- JIT / vmap: JAX compiled and batched execution surfaces.
- CFFGZ: the project bundle format containing template, data, and optional learning state.
- Gauge: redundant numerical coordinate that leaves a belief unchanged.
- Krylov: a matrix-free iterative linear solve using bounded stored vectors.
- D9 / D10: the current factor-local recurrence approximation and the retired whole-model candidate.
- Moment / covariance: an expected feature or product, and its centered second moment.
- Semantic identity: the versioned meaning of a declaration, executable, or persisted statistic.
