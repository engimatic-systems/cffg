CFFG ADVERSARIAL REVIEW: EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Date: 2026-07-10
Branch reviewed: eval-notes at 9471f06, the end of the Gaussian, hybrid,
learning, plates, constraints, and evaluation PR chain.

BOTTOM LINE

CFFG is an unusually strong research prototype, but it is not yet a scalable,
modular inference engine or a trustworthy production policy scorer. I would not
restart it. I would freeze feature breadth while repairing the trust and
execution layers.

The strongest assets are:

1. A clean, JAX-free, serializable graph IR with ports, composition, validation,
   edit actions, and structure hashing.
2. Correct independent parity for ordinary discrete BP/BFE, fixed-parameter
   observation GFE, HMMs, linear Gaussian models, exponential-family conjugacy,
   and the authors' Julia T-maze result.
3. A serious testing culture: 1,091 tests pass; source and test LOC are roughly
   balanced; rule tests often use quadrature, enumeration, or independent
   implementations.
4. A productive experiment loop that has already found diameter, batching,
   readout, and long-horizon limitations.

The highest-risk findings are:

1. cff-hp3y: the new transition-curiosity GFE extension violates cavity
   consistency and includes an incoming-message coupling in node energy. It
   double-counts downstream evidence/preferences. Hallway epistemic-B results
   and GFE-TM energy values must be treated as invalid until rerun.
2. cff-j4mu: Engine.run cannot distinguish converged, budget-exhausted,
   diverged, or infeasible. It does not use free energy as the convergence
   signal and returns poisoned or under-converged states normally.
3. cff-vamy: .cffgz export silently removes plate specifications and then hashes
   the truncated graph, so integrity checks pass on the wrong model.

The scalability claim is also premature. Against Dynamax on the same M2 Max CPU
and identical posterior tasks, CFFG was 316-399x slower for ordinary HMMs,
623-4,206x slower for a full-diameter HMM, and 74-137x slower for LGSSM
smoothing before CFFG readout. Results were accurate. The problem is execution:
Python dispatch, whole-graph flooding, and globally padded arenas, not the local
probability algebra.

RECOMMENDED ORDER

P0 trust gate:
  cff-hp3y, cff-j4mu, cff-vamy.

P1 engine gate:
  cff-3hgi Gaussian numerical validity;
  cff-ri8m executable factor/rule registration;
  cff-d56d schedule-aware inference;
  cff-44we shape-segmented arenas;
  cff-wbhu jitted run loop after convergence semantics are fixed;
  cff-9xmq balanced fan-out;
  cff-4oq8 behavioral epistemics.

Parallel bounded research:
  execute cff-dfpi as a P1 feasibility spike after cff-hp3y. Derive one exact
  action-dependent linear-Gaussian sensing class and
  one discrete-regime/continuous-state hybrid. Do not attempt universal exact
  nonlinear or mixture GFE.

WHAT TO ABANDON

Abandon the goal of one universal exact message rule for arbitrary continuous,
nonlinear, and hybrid factors. General nonlinear mutual information and Gaussian
mixture entropy do not have universal closed forms. Abandon the near-term claim
that CFFG is a commodity continual-learning layer: it currently learns declared
conjugate parameters in a fixed ontology, not representations or structure.
Abandon performance claims based only on JAX, vmap, or a successful 100k stream;
the algorithmic schedule still dominates relevant chains.

WHAT IS WORTH TOKENS

Spend frontier-model tokens on narrowly stated derivations with independent
oracles: action-dependent Gaussian sensing, switching/regime epistemics,
parameter information gain, cavity-consistent messages, and explicit FE bounds.
Spend engineering tokens on schedules, segmented storage, executable rule
contracts, status-bearing runs, and vertical benchmarks. Use LLMs as model and
template authors around a deterministic verifier, not as the source of truth for
the math they also implement and test.

COMMERCIAL VERDICT

There is a plausible commercial wedge, but it is not "factor graphs for
everything." The credible wedge is active diagnostics, adaptive sensing,
system identification, or constrained low-data control where uncertainty,
inspection cost, model auditability, and online updates are valuable. The
generic market already contains PGMax, RxInfer, Dynamax, GTSAM, River, Bayes
Server/Bayesia, and VERSES Genius. CFFG must win one vertical benchmark and make
its constraint/GFE audit trail materially better than those alternatives.

Current commercial readiness: 2/10.
Technical option value if the P0/P1 gates land: 6/10.

WORKFLOW VERDICT

Research velocity: 8/10.
Artifact and handoff discipline: 8/10.
Independent risk control: 4/10.
Overall: 6/10.

The restart-and-handoff pattern works. The failure is common-mode validation:
the same model can write a derivation, implementation, test oracle, docs, and
ticket closure. Make math tickets cross-context and cross-model by construction,
and stop every 3-5 commits for an adversarial merge gate instead of reviewing a
52-commit chain at the end.
