QUESTION 6: TICKET PRIORITIES

VERDICT

The backlog contains many good tickets, but it is ordered as feature expansion
around an engine whose trust, extension, and execution contracts are not yet
stable. Continuous/hybrid epistemics is under-prioritized as research, while
neural factors and some breadth/refactor work are over-prioritized relative to
the current risks.

RECOMMENDED QUEUE

P0: RESULTS ARE NOT TRUSTWORTHY UNTIL THESE LAND

1. cff-hp3y transition-GFE cavity and ledger correction.
2. cff-j4mu converged/exhausted/diverged/infeasible runtime result with FE
   convergence.
3. cff-vamy preserve plates and nested blobs in .cffgz.

All experiments using epistemic_b must be rerun after cff-hp3y. cff-2gev now
correctly depends on it; annealing before correcting the objective would hide a
math defect behind calibration.

P1: STABILIZE THE ENGINE BEFORE MORE FACTOR FAMILIES

1. cff-3hgi Gaussian/hybrid numerical propriety and scale invariance.
2. cff-ri8m executable factor kernel/registry.
3. cff-d56d topology-aware schedules.
4. cff-44we segmented/ragged arenas.
5. cff-wbhu jitted run loop, after cff-j4mu fixes semantics.
6. cff-9xmq balanced Broadcast topology.
7. cff-4oq8 behavioral epistemics suite.

These should form one engine-hardening milestone with explicit dependencies so
they do not race incompatible State, scheduler, and registry refactors.

P1 IN PARALLEL: BOUNDED RESEARCH, NOT PLATFORM IMPLEMENTATION

cff-dfpi is now a high-priority feasibility spike, blocked on cff-hp3y. Cooper
is right that continuous/discrete epistemics could
unlock important uses. The spike should be deliberately narrow:

  exact fixed/action-dependent linear-Gaussian sensing;
  negative additive-control case;
  discrete-regime hybrid over a continuous model;
  decision on exact vs bounded vs sampled FE.

This research can proceed while schedule/registry work lands, but it should not
merge a new factor until cff-hp3y and cff-ri8m establish the correctness and
extension contracts.

P2: PRODUCTIVE API WORK AFTER THE GATE

  cff-x0sv generic learnables, coordinated with cff-ri8m;
  cff-lquu hybrid sufficient statistics/predictive readout;
  cff-cj5h epistemic diagnostics;
  cff-gul8 engine EIG readout;
  cff-mjku fan-out/observation ergonomics;
  cff-j7mm resource estimator;
  cff-6s31 non-materialized plates after schedule/storage design.

cff-gul8 may become P1 if active sensing is chosen as the commercial wedge.

P3: DEFER UNTIL A VERTICAL REQUIRES THEM

  cff-o7hs general nonlinear EP beyond the research spike;
  cff-1o72 neural factors and implicit differentiation;
  cff-pyy9 broad hierarchical/contingent planning;
  cff-iwgl full planning-to-A IS message;
  cff-gjjk learned goal hyperprior;
  visualization polish;
  glucose optimization before a CFFG model and comparison protocol exist.

The full Part II mu1/mu3 messages matter for paper completeness, but they are not
the shortest path to a commercially useful engine. Keep the M3 epic honest and
open; do not let it block a vertical that only needs fixed goals and perceptual
conjugate learning.

REFACTOR ORDER WARNING

cff-l6i5 wants to move Cat/Dir/Gaussian onto the EF abstraction. That could be a
good internal unification, but it does not solve executable registration by
itself. Design cff-ri8m first, then use cff-l6i5 as a migration test. Otherwise
the repository risks doing two large zero-behavior refactors in succession.

PRIORITY PRINCIPLE

Optimize the next six weeks for falsifiable trust and a vertical benchmark, not
for the number of factor families. A smaller engine that reports failure and
runs fast is more valuable than a universal-looking engine whose objective,
termination, or serialization can be wrong silently.
