QUESTION 3: CURIOSITY TESTING AND MATHEMATICAL TRACTABILITY

DO THE TESTS ESTABLISH GENUINE CURIOSITY?

For the fixed observation-GFE path, yes, within a narrow model class.

The strongest evidence is not a local algebra test:

1. The known-A T-maze matches the authors' Julia/LAIF control posterior.
2. With the P-substitution bead, the first action seeks an unrewarded cue and
   the second-step posterior favors the reward arms. Removing the bead skips
   the cue.
3. Across repeated trials with unknown A, the GFE agent learns the cue mapping
   and wins; the BFE ablation largely does not. This is realized behavior, not
   only a negative entropy term.

That is meaningful curiosity-driven behavior.

WHAT THE TESTS DO NOT YET ESTABLISH

1. Relevance: an equally uncertain but reward-irrelevant sensor should not win.
2. Satiation: after a useful observation, repeat-sense value should collapse and
   exploitation should begin.
3. Calibration: sensing should cross a hand-computed Bayes-optimal threshold as
   its cost changes.
4. Realizable information: transition novelty is scored as if the latent next
   state were observed. Its value should weaken with sensor noise, or be labeled
   explicitly as an upper bound.
5. Robust task return: RockSample's GFE arm checked on every step, scored -1 and
   0% success, while no-GFE reached 62.5% success. The experiment omitted check
   cost from preferences, so this is partly a modeling failure, but a mature
   epistemics suite should catch it before celebrating information seeking.
6. Independent transition-GFE behavior: cff-hp3y currently invalidates the
   engine-level GFE-TM path and its FE values.

cff-4oq8 specifies relevance, satiation, cost crossover, sensor quality, return,
and ablation tests.

IS CONTINUOUS/HYBRID GFE TRACTABLE FOR FRONTIER MODELS?

The right answer depends on scope.

Tractable and worth spending tokens on:

1. Fixed linear-Gaussian sensing. For q(z)=N(m,S), x|z=N(Cz,R), and a Gaussian
   goal, predictive q(x) is Gaussian, mutual information is

     I(x;z) = 0.5 * (logdet(C S C' + R) - logdet R),

   and Gaussian goal cross-entropy is closed form. The remaining stationary
   optimization over mean and SPD covariance is difficult but ordinary. It can
   be checked with 1D quadrature, automatic differentiation, and finite
   differences.
2. Action-dependent sensing C_u or R_u. This makes information gain policy
   dependent and gives a purposeful-exploration positive control.
3. A discrete regime selector over continuous Gaussian dynamics. Epistemics can
   stay on the discrete regime while Gaussian messages carry state inference.
4. Conjugate unknown linear parameters, one family at a time, with explicit
   Student-t or variational approximations where prediction leaves Gaussianity.

Not universally tractable, regardless of model capability:

1. Arbitrary nonlinear mutual information.
2. Exact entropy of a general Gaussian mixture.
3. A universal exact message for every factor x constraint configuration.
4. High-dimensional nonconjugate parameter messages without quadrature, Monte
   Carlo, Laplace, or a declared variational bound.

The frontier model can derive a specified class and produce executable checks.
It cannot make a nonexistent closed form exist. Ask it for assumptions, bound
direction, cavity semantics, and an independent oracle before code.

LITERATURE POSITION

The repository's "open problem" label is too broad.

Koudahl, Kouw, and de Vries show standard additive-control linear-Gaussian EFE
does not yield purposeful exploration, but also discuss epistemic objectives and
multiplicative/switching controls that can explore:
  https://wmkouw.github.io/publication/efe-glds/

Collis et al. (2024) learn recurrent switching LDS abstractions and put a
discrete active-inference planner above a continuous controller, explicitly
lifting information-theoretic exploration to discrete space:
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.01066

This supports the project's discrete-regime hybrid route. It does not supply a
CFFG continuous-GFE message table.

A 2026 preprint applies a pragmatic-plus-information objective to constrained
system identification, active search, and optimization, suggesting useful
commercial tasks but not solving CFFG message derivation:
  https://alphaxiv.org/abs/2602.06104v1

RECOMMENDATION

Treat cff-dfpi as a bounded P1 research spike, after the
transition math is repaired. Require one negative linear-Gaussian control, one
positive action-dependent sensing case, one discrete-regime hybrid, and an
explicit decision about exact, bounded, or sampled ledger semantics.
