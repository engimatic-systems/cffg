QUESTION 4: COMMERCIAL VALUE

VERDICT

This is not merely a novelty, but "commodity continual learning" is too broad a
commercial thesis for the artifacts that exist. There is a plausible narrow
product around adaptive sensing, diagnostics, system identification, and
constrained decision-making in low-data systems. The engine must earn that wedge
with vertical outcomes and production trust, not with active-inference theory.

WHY THERE MAY BE VALUE

1. Many operational systems have an authored state ontology: machines, devices,
   protocols, inspections, scientific experiments, and process-control loops.
   CFFG does not need to learn raw representations to be useful there.
2. Online conjugate updates are deterministic, cheap, auditable, and naturally
   paired with active data acquisition.
3. Constraints, uncertainty, goals, and information gain in one inspectable
   model can be valuable when actions or measurements cost money.
4. The Template/edit/bundle design could let an LLM propose model changes while
   a deterministic compiler validates structure, resource cost, inference, and
   policy. That is a credible LLM-plus-symbolic architecture.
5. The active-vision experiment's +27 percentage-point EIG advantage at eight
   patches illustrates a product-shaped benefit, even though EIG was external
   NumPy and the inference throughput missed its bar.

WHY THE GENERAL THESIS IS NOT YET DEFENSIBLE

1. Current "continual learning" means accumulating conjugate counts or Gaussian
   sufficient statistics in a fixed graph. It is not representation learning,
   structural learning, concept-drift handling, or a solution to catastrophic
   forgetting.
2. Runtime inference is 2-4 orders of magnitude slower than specialized JAX
   smoothers on representative chains.
3. The extension contract is not yet public or executable. Customers cannot add
   a new factor without central engine work.
4. Policy trust is not production-grade: termination status is absent, one new
   curiosity derivation is wrong, and plated bundles silently truncate.
5. Deep contingency and large tabular state spaces remain combinatorial.

THE MARKET IS NOT EMPTY

PGMax provides scalable differentiable discrete loopy BP in JAX:
  https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v25/23-1010.html

RxInfer is a mature reactive message-passing ecosystem:
  https://docs.rxinfer.com/

Dynamax provides specialized JAX state-space inference and learning:
  https://probml.github.io/dynamax/

GTSAM provides mature incremental continuous factor-graph inference for sensor
fusion and robotics:
  https://gtsam.org/docs/

River covers practical online/incremental ML and concept drift:
  https://github.com/online-ml/river

Bayes Server and Bayesia already sell Bayesian-network reasoning, diagnostics,
decision automation, and deployment APIs:
  https://www.bayesserver.com/docs/
  https://www.bayesia.com/

VERSES Genius explicitly markets POMDPs, continuous learning, active inference,
model tooling, hosted deployment, and diagnostics:
  https://www.verses.ai/genius

These products validate demand but remove "nobody has this" as a moat.

RECOMMENDED WEDGE

Pick one expensive-observation workflow where the customer controls the model
ontology and can measure avoided cost:

  adaptive industrial diagnostics or inspection sequencing;
  constrained system identification / experimental design;
  sensor scheduling for a device or digital twin;
  low-data anomaly triage with active follow-up measurements.

Sell the outcome and audit trail. The engine can be open source; paid value can
sit in model tooling, connectors, deployment, monitoring, verified factor packs,
and domain templates.

GO/NO-GO EVIDENCE BEFORE A PLATFORM BET

1. Fix all P0 trust tickets.
2. Demonstrate a custom factor without central engine edits.
3. Get within 10-20x of specialized inference on the important vertical and win
   end-to-end because CFFG offers decisions/learning those routines do not.
4. Beat strong task baselines on at least two external benchmarks with costs,
   regret, calibration, and wall-clock included.
5. Show a month-scale streaming deployment with versioned model updates,
   status-bearing inference, deterministic replay, and intact bundle restore.
6. Have a domain expert, not an agent that wrote the code, audit the model and
   objective.

Current commercial readiness: 2/10.
Commercial option value after trust/performance gates: 6/10.
